Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains how personal data is handled when you visit this website and when you use the ThirdBrain-PII connector, under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

1. Controller and contact

Controller: the developer of ThirdBrain-PII, legal name to be completed.
Contact for any privacy request: scrvlr80@gmail.com.

2. What data is processed, why, and on what legal basis

DataPurposeLegal basis (art. 6 GDPR)
Technical data of your visit (IP address, browser, page requested, time), recorded by the hosting provider GitHubDelivering the website and keeping it secureLegitimate interest, art. 6(1)(f): a website cannot be delivered without it
Your email address and the content of your message, if you write to us (for example with “Request access”)Answering you and giving access to the connectorSteps at your request before a contract, art. 6(1)(b), or legitimate interest in answering, art. 6(1)(f)
Claude's answer, holding placeholders only, received by the connector server through the tool thirdbrain_continueShowing the answer in the panel, where your browser puts the real data backPerformance of the service you asked for, art. 6(1)(b). It is not kept or logged

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and loads nothing from other sites.

What you write in the ThirdBrain-PII panel is processed in your own browser. The real values, the mapping between real values and placeholders, and the documents you add to the knowledge stay in your browser's storage on your device. They are not sent to the developer. What you choose to send to Claude (the protected copy) is processed by Anthropic under its own terms and privacy policy.

3. Recipients and transfers outside the EU

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. (United States). GitHub processes the technical data of your visit as an independent provider. Transfers to the United States rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where GitHub is certified, and on the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission. GitHub's privacy statement: docs.github.com.

The connector server runs on Google Cloud Run, region europe-west1 (Belgium). Emails are handled by Google (Gmail). No data is sold or given to anyone else.

4. How long data is kept

  • Visit data: kept by GitHub according to its own policy; the developer does not receive or keep it.
  • Emails: for as long as needed to answer and to manage your access, and no longer than 24 months after the last contact, unless the law requires otherwise.
  • Claude's answer sent to thirdbrain_continue: not kept; it is used only to answer that request.
  • Data in your browser: until you delete it (“Forget all the knowledge” in the panel, or by clearing the browser data).

5. Your rights

Under articles 15 to 21 GDPR you have the right to: access your data (art. 15); have it corrected (art. 16); have it erased (art. 17); restrict its processing (art. 18); receive it in a portable format (art. 20); and object to processing based on legitimate interest (art. 21). Write to scrvlr80@gmail.com; we answer within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live or work, or where the infringement took place (art. 77). In Italy this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

6. No automated decisions

No decision with legal or similarly significant effects is taken about you by automated means, including profiling (art. 22). The panel's automatic recognition of personal data runs on your device to protect your own text; you can change every choice it makes.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
