Name lists

data/names.json is built from public lists of first names and surnames (sources in data/NAMES-SOURCES.md) and from lists prepared with AI assistance (data/names-src/), filtered against Hunspell dictionaries (licenses in data/vocab-sources.json). Dutch and Belgian first names and surnames also come from Wikidata (CC0 1.0, no attribution required): data/names-src/wikidata-nl.txt, built by scripts/names-nl-wikidata.mjs. Dutch and Belgian villages in data/places.json also come from Wikidata (CC0 1.0): data/places-src/wikidata-nl.txt, built by scripts/places-nl-wikidata.mjs. The names of illnesses in data/health.json (8 languages) come from Wikidata too (CC0 1.0): data/health-src/, built by scripts/build-health.mjs.
